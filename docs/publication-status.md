## What the manuscript represents

The manuscript focuses on the hydro-spatial foundation of the work, including the earlier terrain/hydrology/waterlogging research scope represented by that study.

---

## What it does not represent

The manuscript should **not** be treated as a complete description of the current NagarBadh Drishtm implementation.

Subsequent work expanded the project into areas including:

- Detailed drainage-network modelling
- EPA SWMM hydraulic simulation
- Hydraulic outputs and debugging
- Event/scenario inundation
- Road-accessibility thresholds
- SIH-oriented nowcasting workflow
- Dashboard integration

---

## Why the distinction matters

This repository has a broader scope than the manuscript.

The repository documents the evolution of the project.

The manuscript documents a specific research stage.

The dashboard demonstrates the implemented software/prototype.

Keeping these scopes separate avoids overstating the publication record.
